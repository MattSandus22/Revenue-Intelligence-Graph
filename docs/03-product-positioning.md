# 3. Product Positioning

## 3.1 Positioning statement

> **Revenue Intelligence Graph is the evidence-backed account truth layer that continuously detects, explains, and operationalizes revenue risk and growth signals across CRM, product, support, billing, and customer interactions.**

For: B2B SaaS revenue organizations ($10M–$250M ARR) who must retain and expand existing revenue.
Who are: burned by opaque health scores, fragmented account data, and surprise churn.
RIG is: an account-level intelligence graph with an auditable evidence system.
Unlike: CS platforms, conversation intelligence, forecasting tools, and BI stacks —
RIG: unifies all revenue-relevant sources into one canonical account graph, explains every score and alert with cited source evidence, flags when its own data is unreliable, and routes human-approved actions into existing workflow tools.

## 3.2 Category narrative (narrow, initial)

We deliberately do **not** launch as "revenue operating system" (too broad, incumbent-owned) or "AI copilot for CS" (low trust, crowded). Initial category:

> **Renewal Risk Intelligence** — "Know which renewals are at risk, why, and what to do — with evidence you can defend to your board."

The category claim we own: **"evidence-backed."** Every competitor shows scores; only RIG contractually refuses to show a claim it cannot cite. This is a product architecture claim (verification layer, evidence cards, data-reliability score), not a marketing claim, and it's demonstrable in a 15-minute demo: click any score, any alert, any sentence of the executive brief → see the sources.

## 3.3 Message architecture

| Audience | Headline | Proof points |
|---|---|---|
| VP CS | "Stop being surprised by churn." | Risk detected N days earlier; surprise-churn rate; alert precision users can rate |
| RevOps | "One account truth layer you don't have to build." | Identity resolution with confidence + human correction; data-quality command center; connector health |
| CRO/CEO | "A retention forecast you can defend." | Risk-adjusted renewal forecast with calibration history; evidence-linked exec brief |
| Security | "AI you can audit." | No-training default, tenant isolation, citation enforcement, full audit log, human-approval gates |

## 3.4 The three-word test

If a prospect remembers three things: **Graph. Evidence. Action.**
1. **Graph** — one resolved account truth across all systems.
2. **Evidence** — every insight explains itself and cites sources; the system tells you when its own data is unreliable.
3. **Action** — insights become owned, tracked, approved interventions whose outcomes are measured.

## 3.5 Land-and-expand arc (positioning over time)

1. **Land:** Renewal Risk Intelligence (CS + RevOps).
2. **Expand 1:** Account planning & executive reporting (VP CS → CRO/CEO surface area).
3. **Expand 2:** Expansion intelligence (CS + AM + sales leadership).
4. **Expand 3:** Forecast reliability & pipeline risk (VP Sales).
5. **Expand 4:** Data Quality Command Center as a standalone RevOps value prop.
6. **Expand 5:** Broader revenue operating system — only once 1–5 earn the right.

Each expansion reuses the same graph, evidence system, and workflow engine; expansions are new lenses on existing infrastructure, not new products.
